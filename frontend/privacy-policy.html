Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

BathRemodelHub ("BathRemodelHub," "we," "us," or "our") operates this website to help homeowners connect with independent, local bathroom remodeling contractors ("Pros"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Information you provide: your ZIP code, and if you request a quote, your name, phone number, email address, and details about your project.
  • Automatically collected information: IP address, approximate location (derived from your ZIP code, IP address, or device GPS if you grant permission), browser type, device type, pages viewed, and referring pages.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: we use cookies, pixels, and tags (including Google Tag Manager and advertising pixels) to measure site performance and ad effectiveness.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To match you with local Pros who service your area and match your project needs.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the site and our services.
  • To measure and improve our advertising campaigns.
  • To communicate with you about your quote request.
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or technical issues.

3. How We Share Your Information

When you submit a quote request, we share the information you provide (such as your ZIP code and contact details) with one or more Pros or partner networks (such as Thumbtack) so they can contact you about your project. We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may also share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the site (e.g. hosting, analytics, advertising measurement).
  • Advertising partners (e.g. Google, Meta) in aggregated or pseudonymous form to measure ad performance.
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect our rights and users.

4. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember your ZIP code between pages during your session, understand how visitors use the site, and measure the performance of our marketing campaigns. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may affect some site functionality.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • You may decline to provide certain information, though this may limit our ability to match you with a Pro.
  • You may opt out of location-based auto-fill by declining browser location permission.
  • Depending on your state of residence, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to opt out of certain sharing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for users 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email us at support@bathremodelhub.com.
